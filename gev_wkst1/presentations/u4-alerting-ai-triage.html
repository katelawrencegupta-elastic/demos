    Noisy alert · elasticco-noisy-node-cpu

    Custom threshold on host CPU percent — fires on any demo host. No service, tenant, or deploy context.

    Why it fails on-call

    • Metric: avg system.cpu.total.norm.pct > 0.25
    • Scope: all hosts with labels.demo: elastic-co
    • No service.name, tenant.id, or deploy version
    • No Cases action — alert stops at notification
