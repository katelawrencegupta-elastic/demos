    Noisy alert · elasticco-noisy-node-cpu

    Custom threshold on pod CPU nanocores — fires on any demo pod. No service, tenant, or deploy context.

    Why it fails on-call

    • Metric: avg kubernetes.pod.cpu.usage.nanocores > 70
    • Scope: all pods with labels.demo: elastic-co
    • No service.name, tenant.id, or deploy version
    • No Cases action — alert stops at notification
