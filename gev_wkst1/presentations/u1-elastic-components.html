    Unstructured orchestrator logs → structured, searchable, correlated

    Airflow-style orchestrator lines arrive as free text. Elasticsearch ingest pipelines parse them into ECS keyword fields at write time. Kibana Discover filters on tenant.id and trace.id without regex.
